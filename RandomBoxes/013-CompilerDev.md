LLVM is a great platform for compiler research. It has a simple and easy to understand [representation](docs/LangRef.html) and a completely [modular system for adding passes to the compiler](docs/WritingAnLLVMPass.html). LLVM also provides extensive assertions to pinpoint bugs when they occur, an IR verifier, and [tools to automatically identify and narrow down bugs](docs/CommandGuide/html/bugpoint.html) in arbitrary compiler transformations.
