## Student Travel Grants Available

Posted 21 Jan 2017

[![](http://llvm.org/devmtg/2017-03//assets/images/gradschoolCSText.jpg)](#)

Details for the Student Travel Grants are available!

There will be two deadlines to apply, February 1st and March 1st. Notification will be send out at the 15th of the respective month.

All students (including PhD students) are eligible! Please see the [guideline](http://llvm.org/foundation/documents/grant-program/LLVM%20Foundation%20Travel%20Grant%20Guidelines%20and%20Application%20\(08.12.16\).pdf) for details and send the application as well as questions to the [LLVM Foundation Board of Directors (board@llvm.org)](mailto:board@llvm.org).
