# Schedule

At FOSDEM 2018, LLVM will again participate with a dedicated devroom. The devroom at FOSDEM provides a great opportunity for LLVM developers and the wider open source community to get together, connect and discuss.

As possibly the largest European Open Source Conference, FOSDEM takes place in Brussels and attracts more than 650 lectures and over 8000 hackers - many core contributors of the worlds leading open source projects.

The schedule and full details of presentations can be found on the [FOSDEM website](https://fosdem.org/2018/schedule/track/llvm_toolchain/).

# Logistics

The mailing list [llvm-devroom@lists.fosdem.org](mailo:llvm-devroom@lists.fosdem.org) can be used to discuss issues of general interest related to the conference organization.

Kristof Beyls and Arnaud de Grandmaison are organizing this devroom, with help from many others.

The program committee that evaluated the submitted proposals was composed of: Simon Cook, Alex Bradbury, Diana Picus, Jonas Devlieghere, Jeroen Dobbelaere and Kristof Beyls.

# Registration

FOSDEM does not require any registration and is free of charge. However, just like last year, a large crowd must be expected.
